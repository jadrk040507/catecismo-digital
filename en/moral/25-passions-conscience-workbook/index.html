Workbook — The Passions and Moral Conscience

Student Workbook

To use after class or in group discussion.

Remembering the Essentials

  1. What are passions according to the Catechism? Are they good or bad in themselves?
  2. What is moral conscience, and why is it called the «inner sanctuary»?
  3. What are the three states of conscience (certain, doubtful, erroneous), and how should we act in each?
  4. How can passions cloud the judgment of conscience?
  5. What does it mean for conscience to be «formed»? How is it formed?

For Reflection and Writing

1. Which emotion do you most struggle to control? In what situations does it get away from you?

 

2. Have you ever done something you knew was wrong because you «couldn’t help it»? What happened after?

 

3. How do you form your conscience? What sources do you use to know what is good and what is evil?

 

4. Is there an area of your life where you know your conscience is asking you to change, but you haven’t yet?

 

5. How does prayer and confession help you order your passions and form your conscience?

 

Practical Activities

A. Emotions and Conscience Journal

For one week, keep a journal where you note:

  • A situation where you felt a strong emotion (anger, fear, joy, sadness)
  • How you responded to that emotion
  • Whether your conscience approved or disapproved of your response
  • What you would do differently next time

B. Ignatian Examen of Conscience

Practice St. Ignatius’s Examen each night for 5 minutes using these questions:

  1. What am I grateful for today?
  2. What happened today? (review the day like a movie)
  3. Where did I feel a strong emotion? How did I respond?
  4. Where did I fail? Where did I succeed?
  5. What will I do tomorrow?

C. The Art of Pausing

This week, before responding to an emotionally intense situation, pause for 5 seconds. Breathe deeply. Ask yourself: «What does my conscience tell me about this?» Then respond. Write about the experience.

Memory Verse

«Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God»

Matthew 5:8

Prayer for This Week

Lord Jesus,
You who know my heart better than I do,
help me to order my passions.
May anger not govern my words.
May fear not paralyze my faith.
May desire not cloud my reason.
Form my conscience with Your Word,
so that I may distinguish good from evil
and have a pure heart that sees You.
Amen.
